# Sky textures

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for Zoen; no Hark artwork is copied. The user authorized observing Hark on their Samsung as a visual reference: a tightly cropped, heavily defocused sky with low contrast.

The shipped assets are `apps/zoen/assets/sky/day.jpg` and `night.jpg`, each1024×1536. The unused earlier `warm.jpg` study texture has been removed from the source assets. There are only two sky families. Release bundle inspection remains a separate packaging check. The original generated PNGs were encoded as JPEG quality86; rendering handles the enlarged crop and native20-point blur. The app uses only a quiet part of each texture, with no landscape or prominent cloud outlines. No network image request is required.

Each exact generation prompt was the common prefix followed by the corresponding suffix below.

## Common prefix

Create one production photographic texture asset for a refined iPhone messaging app background. Portrait 1024x1536. This is ONLY a photorealistic photograph of a SMALL ZOOMED-IN PATCH of sky, shot with a long telephoto lens looking upward; NOT a landscape or an illustration or a phone mockup. Close crop, no horizon, no earth, no buildings, no birds, no text, no sun or moon disk, no mountain, no water, no lens flare, no dramatic composition, no gradient wallpaper look, no purple, no magenta. The framing feels like a little quiet fragment of real sky enlarged. Natural detailed cloud texture, subtle low contrast, tasteful and restrained, many calm uncluttered areas suitable behind readable app cards. Keep large cloud forms cropped at the edges rather than filling with repeated little clouds. Natural optics, realistic atmospheric scattering, no artificial swirls.

## day suffix

DAYLIGHT: very pale powder blue and pearl white, extremely gentle color, lightly sunlit feathered edge of one real white cloud entering from the lower right and a delicate wisp near the top. High-key exposure, almost-white sky with enough real fine soft cloud detail to read as a photograph. The blue is faint and airy, never saturated cyan. Calm and premium.

## warm suffix

DAWN / LATE AFTERNOON: high-key pale ivory sky with a delicate warm champagne light on a small cropped cloud edge. Almost-white natural light, a whisper of warm cream, no pink/purple/orange wash. Thin realistic cloud filaments with soft texture, very subtle warm horizon light but NO horizon visible. Gentle, quiet and premium; avoid heavy gray clouds.

## night suffix

NIGHT: a quiet small fragment of real deep blue-black night sky, extremely faint moonlit wispy cloud edges entering from one side. Subtle natural navy and charcoal with very low contrast. No violet, no bright blue, no visible moon, no Milky Way, no dense stars, no dramatic nebula. At most two tiny dim real stars. Clean low-noise photographic detail, calm and premium.
