Read-only review; no files changed. Ranked by impact.

1. **Critical — The hash chain does not prevent server equivocation.**  
   Claim: “signed logs they can’t forge or reorder undetected” — [security.md:16](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:16), [ADR 0002:15](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/adr/0002-event-format-v2.md:15). Authors do not sign `seq` or `prev`. The operator can omit or reorder events, recompute every chain link, and present different valid histories to different devices. An in-memory reproduction passed all documented signature and chain checks after these attacks. **Fix:** add participant-authenticated checkpoints and cross-client consistency checks or independent witnesses; test rehashed forks.

2. **Critical — “Sent means never lost” contradicts DR.**  
   Claims: “No message is lost once the sender sees ‘sent’” versus asynchronous DR with “RPO under 5 s” — [system-design.md:16](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:16), [system-design.md:226](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:226). Regional destruction can lose acknowledged messages, removals, and key-package claims. A network partition can also make asynchronous lag exceed five seconds. **Fix:** acknowledge only after durable replication outside the promised failure domain; specify promotion fencing and MLS recovery. [FDB’s safe multi-region design](https://github.com/apple/foundationdb/wiki/Multi-Region-Replication) depends on surviving synchronous log copies.

3. **Critical — Server-hosted agents invalidate the stated operator threat model.**  
   Claim: a malicious operator gets only E2EE ciphertext — [security.md:16](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:16). But `zoen-agentd` runs on the server, joins as an MLS member, and calls model providers — [plan-real.md:165](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/plan-real.md:165). The operator can extract that agent’s keys and plaintext; providers receive submitted content. **Fix:** use device-side or independently hosted agents for operator-resistant Spaces, or explicitly disclose that server agents and their providers are trusted recipients and narrow the security guarantee.

4. **High — Key transparency is deferred and its proposed checks are insufficient.**  
   Claim: “key substitution is caught by key transparency,” although KT is “Later” — [security.md:16](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:16), [security.md:53](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:53). Inclusion proofs and server-signed epochs alone do not prevent separate valid views. Logging identity-to-device bindings also leaves handle-to-identity substitution unspecified. **Fix:** authenticate the complete lookup chain, pin checkpoints, require witness/gossip consistency, and test valid split views. Until then, require explicit verification for the claimed guarantee.

5. **High — MLS membership and device revocation lack a complete protocol.**  
   Claims: the relay rejects commits whose roster disagrees, and remote revocation removes a device from every group — [plan-real.md:141](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/plan-real.md:141), [security.md:19](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:19). An encrypted commit does not expose its roster to the relay. No atomic binding between commit, membership delta, epoch, Welcome delivery, and revocation is specified. Edge membership caches depend on lossy notifications — [system-design.md:174](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:174). **Fix:** define authenticated public control data, atomic epoch transitions, client validation, and durable revocation delivery with stale-cache rejection. [MLS distinguishes public and encrypted handshake messages](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc9420/).

6. **High — FoundationDB credentials do not provide the claimed least privilege.**  
   Claim: “FDB and Postgres credentials per service” bound lateral movement — [security.md:17](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:17), [security.md:83](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:83). Ordinary trusted FDB clients can access the entire cluster; directories are not authorization boundaries. A compromised worker with connectivity can alter membership, keys, or logs throughout its cell. **Fix:** restrict FDB connectivity to a trusted storage service that enforces operation and prefix permissions. Tenant authorization needs explicit evaluation; it is documented as experimental. [FDB authorization](https://apple.github.io/foundationdb/authorization.html).

7. **High — The FDB hardware and storage budget are wrong.**  
   Claim: 1.8 PB fits on 300 nodes with 6 TB each — [ADR 0008:41](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/adr/0008-foundationdb-system-of-record.md:41). That consumes **100% of raw disk**, before indexes, transaction logs, dedupe, compaction, or recovery headroom. Worse, the recommended 8-vCPU/64-GiB AWS i4i size has **1.875 TB**, not 6 TB: 300 nodes provide only 562.5 TB. **Fix:** specify actual instance types and complete storage accounting; message replicas alone require 960 such nodes at 100% utilization, or about 1,372 at 70%. [AWS specifications](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/instance-types/storage-optimized/).

8. **High — The outbox layout is incomplete for batching and wakeups.**  
   Claims: one `outbox/{partition}/{versionstamp}` entry per message, batches of 50, and watches instead of polling — [system-design.md:117](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:117), [system-design.md:201](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:201), [ADR 0008:25](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/adr/0008-foundationdb-system-of-record.md:25). A batch shares one transaction versionstamp; entries collide without distinct user versions or suffixes. FDB watches observe individual keys, not new keys under a prefix. **Fix:** specify unique batch suffixes and a race-safe wakeup protocol with polling fallback. [Versionstamps](https://apple.github.io/foundationdb/javadoc/com/apple/foundationdb/tuple/Versionstamp.html), [watches](https://apple.github.io/foundationdb/api-python.html#transaction-watch).

9. **High — Losing the final online event never produces a gap.**  
   Claim: “lost frame: client sees a seq gap and pulls” — [system-design.md:139](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:139). If sync dies after commit but before publication, or Core NATS drops the last event, no later sequence arrives to reveal it. A connected recipient can remain stale indefinitely. **Fix:** add independently driven head reconciliation and replay from the committed log; reconcile every subscription reconnect. Explicitly handle reordered post-commit publications too.

10. **High — JetStream capacity and failure isolation are missing.**  
    Claims: seven-day R3 `EV` retention and consumer outages “never” blocking sends — [system-design.md:140](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:140), [system-design.md:206](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:206). Full envelopes require approximately **420 TB replicated**, absent from the cost model. Subjects do not shard a single stream’s Raft group. JetStream shares resources with chat routing; exhausted storage or a growing FDB outbox can affect sends. **Fix:** specify separate stream shards, resource isolation, backlog limits, replay frontiers, and full-disk behavior. [NATS partitioning](https://examples.nats.io/examples/jetstream/partitions/cli/).

11. **High — Recovery and subscription state are not included in sizing.**  
    Claim: a reconnect storm’s 27M head reads/s is served in “about 1 ms per batched read” — [ADR 0009:22](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/adr/0009-fanout-logs-not-inboxes.md:22). This is roughly 38 times peak append rate, before history replay or authentication. At 100 Spaces/device, 160M sockets imply **16B device–Space mappings**. Membership spread across independent cells cannot be listed in one global FDB read batch. **Fix:** design federated membership discovery, versioned head caches, bounded recovery admission, and subscription accounting; benchmark correlated reconnect storms.

12. **High — Presence is persisted despite the privacy guarantee.**  
    Claim: NATS KV presence with a 90-second TTL is “never persisted” — [system-design.md:147](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:147), [system-design.md:172](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:172), [security.md:92](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:92). KV is a JetStream stream; TTL does not establish non-persistence. Maintaining 160M entries requires over **1.78M refreshes/s**, also absent from sizing. **Fix:** use explicitly non-durable, partitioned presence state, or document retention and provision its replication workload. [NATS KV architecture](https://docs.nats.io/learn/key-value/).

13. **High — Idempotency expires, and checkpoints do not protect external effects.**  
    Claims: “Every network operation is idempotent,” dedupe expires after seven days, and jobs checkpoint after tool calls — [system-design.md:17](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:17), [system-design.md:114](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:114), [system-design.md:142](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:142). An acknowledged send retried after eight days can append again. A crash after an external action succeeds but before its checkpoint can repeat that action. **Fix:** align dedupe with an enforced retry horizon; persist operation identities before execution and use provider idempotency or explicit ambiguous-result reconciliation.

14. **High — Retention and cryptographic erasure promises do not match the key lifecycle.**  
    Claims: logs retained 30 days, older events recoverable from cold segments, account deletion makes backups unreadable, and agent removal makes its epoch-encrypted cache unreadable — [security.md:92](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:92), [system-design.md:213](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:213), [security.md:127](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:127), [ADR 0014:28](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/adr/0014-memory-and-knowledge-are-items.md:28). Removing an MLS member does not erase its old exporter keys. Per-region volume encryption does not provide per-account backup erasure. Deleting old commits also needs an offline MLS recovery path. **Fix:** define retention and key destruction for every copy, plus authenticated rejoin/checkpoint recovery; limit revocation guarantees to future access.

15. **High — Per-connection queue caps do not bound node memory.**  
    Claim: a 1 MB queue cap triggers resync “long before memory runs out” — [system-design.md:281](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:281). At 150k connections, queues can consume **150 GB** on a budgeted 32-GB node, before TLS and subscription state. Correlated slow clients defeat the assumption. A saturated queue may also prevent delivery of `Resync`. **Fix:** enforce a node-wide byte budget, reserve control-frame capacity, and disconnect or shed clients before memory exhaustion.

16. **High — The conflict-free counter is not a strict rate limiter.**  
    Claim: `rl/{account}/{minute}` uses “atomic add, no conflicts” — [system-design.md:119](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:119). Atomic add does not return the previous value. Strict read-check-increment introduces conflicts; snapshot checking permits concurrent overshoot. Per-cell counters also multiply an account’s allowance across cells. **Fix:** choose strict serialized enforcement or bounded distributed token allocations, specify overshoot, and define an account-wide quota authority. [FDB atomic-operation semantics](https://apple.github.io/foundationdb/api-python.html#atomic-operations).

17. **Medium — The latency budget does not establish the SLO.**  
    Claim: summed hop p99s yield a 126-ms total with 74-ms headroom — [system-design.md:182](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/system-design.md:182). Summing six p99s does not prove an end-to-end p99; their individual bounds only provide a 94% union-bound guarantee at that sum. Server `before` hooks alone receive 300 ms — [hooks.md:24](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/hooks.md:24). **Fix:** define the eligible delivery population and measure end-to-end latency under failure and overload; constrain synchronous hooks to the same budget.

18. **Medium — Identifier redaction claims already disagree with code.**  
    Claim: identifiers are logged as keyed hashes and a journey scan detects leaks — [security.md:88](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/docs/security.md:88). Sessions log `short(identity)`, which is the first 12 raw characters — [session.rs:118](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/crates/zoen-relay/src/session.rs:118), [session.rs:153](/workspace/zoen-real/repo/crates/zoen-relay/src/session.rs:153). These prefixes remain matchable against the public directory; scanning only complete identifiers misses them. **Fix:** centralize keyed pseudonymization, rotate keys, and test prefixes and serialized metadata as well as complete IDs.

The three changes I would make first:

1. **Repair the security contract:** prevent valid log forks, authenticate directory lookups, and explicitly define server-agent trust.
2. **Make acknowledgment and membership durable:** choose a regional-loss guarantee, implement atomic MLS transitions, and repair lost final notifications.
3. **Validate one complete production cell:** use real hardware and storage budgets, then test reconnect storms, full disks, consumer outages, and removal races.
